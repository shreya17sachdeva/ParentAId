👩‍💻 Bridging Curiosity with Clarity: Meet ParentAId! 🤖✨

Ever been bombarded with "Why is the sky blue?", "Where do stars go during the day?", or even trickier questions from your little ones? As parents, answering these curious minds in a way they truly understand can be challenging. That’s where ParentAId comes in! 🚀

🔹 What is ParentAId?
ParentAId is an AI-powered web application designed to help parents provide sophisticated yet child-friendly answers to their kids' endless questions. Built using the MERN stack and powered by Llama 3 (70B) on Groq Cloud, our platform ensures responses are accurate, engaging, and easy to understand.

🔹 How does it work?
✅ Ask any question your child throws at you
✅ Our AI processes and simplifies the response
✅ You get a well-structured answer—science-backed, age-appropriate, and easy to explain!

🔹 Why ParentAId?
🔍 Bridging the gap between technical accuracy and simplicity
📚 AI-driven insights backed by a custom knowledge base
🌍 Encourages curiosity while making learning fun for kids
